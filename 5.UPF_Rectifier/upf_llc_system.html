5.UPF_Rectifier · system study

Dornbirn UPF rectifier + LLC: 230 Vac to 48 V at 60 W

The UPF rectifier from the ECCE 2024 paper charges a bus of two stacked capacitors. A full-bridge LLC with synchronous rectifier turns the bus into a regulated 48 V. This page explains the whole chain with its formulas, and simulates how the bus voltage behaves from full load down to light load, including load steps. The output is always held at 48 V by the LLC. The bus voltage has to be held by the rectifier's own control loop, and that is where this topology differs from a normal boost PFC.

1. Stage 1: the UPF rectifier sets input power by frequency

From the paper, in IRCM the rectifier looks like a resistor to the grid:

R_in = 8·fs1·√(L1·L2)·(1 − k²) / k (paper eq. 14) P_in = V_m² / (2·R_in) = V_m²·k / (16·fs1·n·L2·(1 − k²)) (paper eq. 20)

The important consequence: input power depends on fs1 and the inductors only, not on the load and not on the bus voltage. With everything else fixed, Pin ∝ 1/fs1. If the design delivers full power at fs1,full, then any other power needs

fs1(P) = fs1,full · P_full / P (IRCM: R_in ∝ fs1)

A boost PFC is different: its current loop draws whatever the load asks for. This rectifier draws a fixed power set by fs1, whatever the load takes. So the difference between what comes in and what goes out piles up in the bus capacitors, and a control loop must adjust fs1 to keep it balanced.

The bus voltage must also respect the paper's conditions, with VC = Vbus/2:

no CCM: V_bus ≥ V_m (|v_in|/2 ≤ V_C) IRCM everywhere (UPF): V_bus ≥ 2·V_m/(nk − 2) (|v_in|/(nk−2) ≤ V_C) no zero-crossing distortion: nk > 2

2. The bus: an energy balance

Cb1 and Cb2 in series form the bus capacitance Cbus = Cb/2. The energy in it changes by the difference between rectifier power and LLC power:

d/dt ( ½·C_bus·V_bus² ) = p_in(t) − P_bus , P_bus = P_o / η_LLC

Three things follow from this one equation.

  1. 100 Hz ripple. With a resistive input, pin(t) = Pin·(1 − cos 2ωt). Its average is Pin, but it pulses at twice the mains frequency. Integrating the pulsating part gives a ripple proportional to the power:
    ΔV_bus,pp ≈ P_bus / (ω·C_bus·V_bus) (ω = 2π·50 Hz)
  2. Open loop runs away. At a fixed fs1, Pin is fixed. If the load drops, the extra power charges the bus with nothing to stop it:
    dV_bus/dt ≈ (P_in − P_bus) / (C_bus·V_bus)
  3. The loop must be slow. If the loop reacted to the 100 Hz ripple, it would modulate fs1 within each mains cycle, and the input current would stop being sinusoidal. So the controller samples the bus energy once per half mains cycle (every 10 ms, averaged) and runs a PI loop with a crossover of about 5 Hz, the usual PFC voltage-loop trade-off. Its output is a power command Pcmd, which sets fs1 = fs1,full·P_full/P_cmd.

Because the loop is slow, a sudden load drop makes the bus overshoot before the loop catches up. A fast overvoltage protection (OVP) stops switching at +8% until the bus falls back. An optional feed-forward adds the LLC's power, which the controller can know from the output side, directly to Pcmd. Then the PI loop only has to trim the losses, and load steps barely move the bus.

3. Light load: out of frequency range, into burst mode

Lower power needs higher fs1. Switching loss, gate drive and magnetics limit it to some fs1,max, which sets the lowest power the rectifier can deliver continuously:

P_min = P_bus,full · fs1,full / fs1,max (continuous operation needs P_bus ≥ P_min)

Below Pmin the rectifier cannot reduce its power any further, so the only way to balance the bus is to stop switching part of the time: burst mode. It runs at Pmin until the bus reaches the top of a hysteresis band, stops, and restarts when the bus falls to the bottom of the band. Starting and stopping on mains half-cycle boundaries keeps each burst a clean half sine.

Burst mode costs power factor. When running, the current is a perfect sine. When stopped, it is zero. With an on fraction D:

P = D·P_min , I_rms = √D·I_rms,on → PF = P / (V_rms·I_rms) = √D

The bus now swings over the whole hysteresis band, set by the burst thresholds, not by the load. That swing is what the LLC has to absorb at light load.

4. Stage 2: the LLC turns the bus into 48 V

The full-bridge LLC is designed to run at resonance at the nominal bus voltage, where it is most efficient. Its turns ratio follows from the unity-gain condition. The tank is sized by the quality factor at full load:

N = V_bus,ref / V_o (full bridge: gain M = N·V_o / V_bus = 1 at resonance) R_ac = 8·N²·R_L / π² , R_L = V_o² / P_o Z0 = Q_full·R_ac , Lr = Z0 / (2π·fr) , Cr = 1 / (2π·fr·Z0) , Lm = Ln·Lr

With first-harmonic approximation (FHA), the gain at normalized frequency fn = fs2/fr is

M(fn) = 1 / √( [1 + (1 − 1/fn²)/Ln]² + Q²·(fn − 1/fn)² ) , Q = Q_full · P_o / P_o,full

The LLC controller picks fn so that M = N·Vo/Vbus. A higher bus needs M < 1 (above resonance), a lower bus needs M > 1 (below resonance). At light load Q → 0, and the gain curve flattens. Even at infinite frequency the gain cannot go below

M_min = Ln / (Ln + 1) → V_bus,max = N·V_o·(Ln + 1)/Ln

This ties the two stages together: at light load the rectifier's burst band and any overshoot must stay below Vbus,max, or the LLC loses regulation and has to burst too. (FHA is only approximate far above resonance and at very light load. It is used here to show the trend and the limit, not exact frequencies.)

6. Assumptions

  • Mains 230 Vrms, 50 Hz. Coupling factor k = 0.9 for the coupled inductor. fs1,full = 100 kHz at full load, fs1,min = 80 kHz, which gives 25% power headroom for load steps.
  • The rectifier is lossless in the bus balance. The LLC efficiency is a constant 95%, so Pbus = Po/0.95. The LLC regulates 48 V instantly (its loop is far faster than the bus loop), so it draws constant power from the bus.
  • Rectifier input current uses the closed-form local average from the THD derivation: exactly vin/Rin in IRCM, and the DCM expression where IRCM is lost (low nk). Switching ripple is not shown.
  • Bus loop: PI on bus energy, sampled every half mains cycle, crossover 5 Hz, zero at a quarter of that. OVP at +8% with 2% hysteresis. Burst band ±6 V around the reference, decided per half cycle.
  • LLC: fr = 250 kHz, Ln = 5, Qfull = 0.4, gain by FHA.
