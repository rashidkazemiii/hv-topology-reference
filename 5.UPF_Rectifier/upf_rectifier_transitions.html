The UPF rectifier with dead time and parasitic capacitances

The paper's model, shown on the Dornbirn UPF Rectifier page, changes V2 at the switching instants and Vx at the zero crossings of i1 in zero time, and it does not draw the dead time. Here each gate turns on a fixed dead time after the other one turns off (2% of T). The switch node carries Coss of S1 and S2. The bridge AC terminals carry the diode capacitance Cj with a damping resistance R. Every voltage step becomes an interval of its own, and i1 and i2 bend with it. The instants of this model are lettered A, B, C, … so they are not confused with the paper's t0–t4.
