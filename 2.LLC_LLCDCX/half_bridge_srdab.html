2.LLC_LLCDCX · half bridge on both sides · TI SDAA372

The half-bridge resonant converter with an active secondary

Two half bridges, one on each side of a transformer, with a series Lr–Cr tank between them. Both bridges switch at a 50 % duty cycle, and the only control inputs are the switching frequency and the phase shift θ between the two bridges. TI calls it a DBSRC / SRDAB. With the transformer's magnetizing inductance included, it is the LLC tank with an active rectifier. This page first explains how one switching period works, on both sides. It then goes through the phase-shift law of the TI application note SDAA372, checks every equation, and corrects what does not hold.

1The circuit

Why a half bridge gives ±V/2. The split capacitors C1, C2 hold Vbus/2 each, and their midpoint is the AC reference. When Q1 is on, node A sits at the top rail, so vA = +Vbus/2 against the midpoint. When Q2 is on, vA = −Vbus/2. The same holds on the secondary: Q3 gives vB = +VBat/2 and Q4 gives −VBat/2. The tank current returns through the capacitor midpoints, so the split capacitors also carry the AC current. If they are small, they can serve as the resonant capacitor.

Seen from the primary, the tank sits between two square waves: vA = ±Vbus/2 and vB′ = vB/n = ±M·Vbus/2, delayed by θ. M = VBat/(n·Vbus) is the voltage ratio. The tank current is set by the difference of the two square waves, and the power by how far vB′ lags vA. This is the same idea as a DAB, with Lr–Cr instead of a plain inductor.

Where Lm sits. Both bridges force their voltages, so Lm is driven directly by vB′. It carries a triangular current that does not change ires. It only changes the secondary current, isec′ = ires − iLm. The application note neglects Lm. Section 6 shows that it actually helps the switch that is hardest to keep in ZVS.

2One switching period, step by step

3What ZVS needs

A switch turns on at zero voltage if, just before its gate goes high, the current is already flowing through its body diode, that is, against its normal direction. That current has also discharged its Coss. On the primary, ires leaves node A, so Q1 needs ires < 0 when it turns on at ωt = 0, and Q2 needs ires > 0 at ωt = π. On the secondary, Q3 turns on at ωt = θ and needs the secondary current positive (isec′ > 0). By symmetry, Q4 at θ + π needs the opposite sign. With the correct FHA current from section 4:

Q1 (primary): i_res(0) = −(1 − M·cos θ) < 0 ⇔ cos θ < 1/M Q3 (secondary): i_res(θ) = M − cos θ > 0 ⇔ cos θ < M both: θ > θ_strict = arccos[min(M, 1/M)] (app note, correct)

For M > 1 (boost), the primary is the weak side. For M < 1 (buck), the secondary is. At M = 1, any θ > 0 gives ZVS on both sides, at least under FHA.

4The app note's equations, checked one by one

The error in eq. 6. Dividing by jXres turns the voltage phasor by −90°. Equation 6 drops the j, which in effect treats the tank as a resistor. Its zero crossing then lands at −70° for M = 1 and θ = 40°, before Q1 even turns on, while the app note's own Figure 1-2 and eq. 10 put it at θ/2 = +20°. The corrected form below is the one the app note silently uses later, in eqs. 11 and 13.
phasors (sin reference): V_A = 1, V_B′ = M·e^(−jθ) (units of 2V_bus/π) I = (V_A − V_B′)/(j·X_res) = [ M·sin θ − j·(1 − M·cos θ) ] / X_res i_res(t) = (2V_bus/(π·X_res)) · [ M·sin θ · sin ωt − (1 − M·cos θ) · cos ωt ] ← corrected eq. 6 tan(ωt_zero) = (1 − M·cos θ) / (M·sin θ) ← corrected eqs. 8–9

5The phase-shift law

What the app note wants. If M ≠ 1, the zero crossing cannot be at θ/2 (eq. 11, which holds). So the note asks for it at λθ, with λ = 1/(1+M), and that gives eq. 13. The problem: for M ≠ 1, eq. 13 has no solution below 90°. Its only root is near θ ≈ 97–98°, past the angle of maximum power, where the circulating current is very large. So eqs. 14–15 are not solutions of eq. 13. They are fits of the ZVS boundary θstrict, scaled by a factor (1 − 0.25·|M − 1|).

That factor is the problem. It pulls θ below the ZVS boundary on the weak side. Eq. 14 is below θstrict for every M ≠ 1, and at M = 1 it gives θ = 0, which means no power. Eq. 15 adds a 0.8 margin inside the arccos, which helps near M = 1. The (1 − 0.25|M − 1|) factor still wins at the ends: the primary loses ZVS for M ≳ 1.6 (FHA), and the secondary is at the boundary at M = 0.5.

corrected law: θ_ZVS(M) = arccos[ c · min(M, 1/M) ], 0 < c < 1 weak-side turn-on current (FHA) = (1 − c) · min(1, M) · (2V_bus/(π·X_res)) > 0 for every M

Drop the (1 − 0.25·|M − 1|) factor and keep the margin c inside the arccos. The weak side then always has a turn-on current of (1 − c)·min(1, M) in per-unit. That is a guaranteed ZVS margin, for the price of a slightly larger θ, which means a bit more RMS current per watt (third chart below).

6Control: θ from M, frequency from power — and what Lm adds

1. measure M = V_Bat/(n·V_bus) → θ = θ_ZVS(M) (ZVS) 2. power demand P* → X_res* = 2M·V_bus²·sin θ/(π²·P*) 3. X_res = Z0·(F − 1/F), Z0 = √(Lr/Cr) → F = fs/fr = [x + √(x² + 4)]/2, x = X_res*/Z0 4. Lm adds to the secondary turn-on current: ΔI_Q3 = (π²·M/8)·(1 − 1/F²)/Ln (per unit, exact for the triangle)

Lm helps the buck side. Just before Q3 turns on, vB′ has been negative for half a period, so iLm is at its negative peak. That adds directly to the current Q3 needs. In buck mode (M < 1) the secondary is the weak side, so a finite Ln lets you use a smaller θ there, which means less circulating current. In boost mode it does nothing for the primary, because ires does not depend on Lm. Try Ln = 4 with M = 0.6 in section 2 and watch the Q3 tile.

7Limits

  • Dead time and Coss are neglected, as in the app note. A real ZVS check also needs the turn-on current to be large enough to swing Coss within the dead time: I ≥ 2Coss·V/tdead. The margin c should be chosen to meet that at the lowest power.
  • The exact waveforms are exact for this ideal linear circuit: square-wave sources, Lr–Cr and Lm. They include all harmonics, which FHA does not. That is why the exact and FHA ZVS currents differ, most of all close to resonance.
  • The app note's PLECS results (24 V / 40–60 V, 300 W) are not reproduced here, because its tank values and turns ratio are not given.
